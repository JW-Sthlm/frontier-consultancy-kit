# Business Transformation

AI-first delivery changes the economics of partner work. This track helps partners redesign offers, capture value, and build the organization needed to support AI-resilient pricing.

## AI Transformation for partners workshop

Choose the route that fits your practice:

- **Interactive workshop:** work with a facilitator on a live offer, practice, or customer motion. Contact [Giel Cuijpers](mailto:gielcuijpers@microsoft.com) to discuss a session.
- **Self-serve workshop:** <a href="https://aka.ms/aitp-cpe" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://aka.ms/aitp-cpe (opens in a new tab)</a>. Work independently or with your team, then bring your commercial hypothesis to review.

Start with the [value capture and pricing exercise](exercise/value-capture-and-pricing-models.md).

## Pricing strategies exercise: Invoice Processing

Explore pricing strategies for a fictitious Invoice Processing solution using the [case-study presentation](https://jw-sthlm.github.io/frontier-consultancy-kit/business/exercise/ppt/Pricing%20strategies%20-%20Invoice%20Processing%20case%20study.pptx). Compare cost-plus, subscription, output-based, outcome-based, and hybrid proposals. Recommend a model with clear assumptions, customer value, partner margin, and allocation of risk.

## Organizing for the new model

Outcome pricing needs more than a new rate card. Read [Organizing to support the new business model](organizing/) for the measurement instruments, value discipline, and organizational capabilities that need to exist before an outcome deal closes.

## Important boundary

The pricing patterns are illustrative. They are not financial advice, a rate card, or a promise of customer outcomes. Validate assumptions with your finance, legal, procurement, industry, and delivery teams.
