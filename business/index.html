Pricing strategies exercise: Invoice Processing

Explore how to price a fictitious Invoice Processing solution. Use the case-study presentation as your starting point, then compare the economics and risks of alternative commercial models.

Compare pricing strategies

Test cost-plus, subscription, output-based, outcome-based, and hybrid proposals against the same case assumptions. Separate implementation charges from recurring fees and distinguish processed invoices from measured customer outcomes.

Make the commercial case

Use the figures in the presentation to estimate partner margin and customer value. Discuss what changes when invoice volume, processing cost, or solution performance differs from expectations.

Your exercise output

Recommend a pricing model, state your assumptions, and explain who carries each risk. Define the baseline, success measure, attribution rule, and review points needed before making an outcome commitment.

Outcome pricing needs an organization behind it.

Before the first outcome deal closes, the partner needs measurement instruments, value measurement discipline, and organizational requirements that can hold the promise.

Measure and discipline

Use a Value Ledger and Synthetic FTE model. Set IST versus SOLL for every KPI and price the activity that creates the outcome.

Organize and assure

Build Value Assurance, develop industry focus, and use BPR or advisory to prove process value before pricing the result.
