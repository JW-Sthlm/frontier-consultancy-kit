# Business Transformation workshop

Business Transformation is a commercial working session for partners whose delivery model is changing as AI compresses effort.

Use the [value capture and pricing models exercise](value-capture-and-pricing-models.md) to:

1. Choose a service activity that AI may change.
2. Record the current baseline and the customer outcome.
3. Model human, agent, and Services IP inputs.
4. Select a pricing pattern to test in a lighthouse pilot.
5. Identify the organizational capability needed to assure the result.

## Pricing strategies exercise: Invoice Processing

Use the [case-study presentation](https://jw-sthlm.github.io/frontier-consultancy-kit/business/exercise/ppt/Pricing%20strategies%20-%20Invoice%20Processing%20case%20study.pptx) to explore pricing for a fictitious Invoice Processing solution.

1. Record the case assumptions from the presentation. Label any additional assumptions separately.
2. Compare cost-plus, subscription, output-based, outcome-based, and hybrid proposals using the same baseline.
3. Separate implementation and recurring charges. Estimate partner margin and customer value without counting the same cost or benefit twice.
4. Test changes in invoice volume, processing cost, and solution performance. Identify who carries the risk in each model.
5. Recommend a model and define its success measure, attribution rule, and review points.

Deliver a comparison of the models and a short recommendation. The case is fictitious; its figures are not a rate card or a customer commitment.
