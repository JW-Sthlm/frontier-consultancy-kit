Frequently asked

Frontier Partner badge — Q&A.

QHow do I enroll in the Frontier Partner badge?

Partners should focus efforts on meeting the Solutions Partner designation and specialization requirements. Microsoft will contact the MAICPP Partner Admins for qualified partners within 90 days of qualification within Partner Center. There is no enrollment currently available in Partner Center.

QWho is eligible for the Frontier Partner badge?

Any partner who can attain the required Solutions Partner designation and specializations is eligible for the Frontier Partner badge.

QWhat are the requirements to earn the Frontier Partner badge?

Partners must achieve three specializations: Microsoft Copilot; Build AI Apps, AI Platform or Accelerate Developer Productivity; and Data Security. Partners must also attain Solutions Partner designations in Modern Work or Business Applications; Security; and one of the three Cloud & AI Platform aligned designation.

QWhat steps should I take to qualify?

Attain the required specializations and Solutions Partner designations. Check the status of your Solutions Partner designations and specialization enrollments in the Membership workspace in Partner Center. You will need MAICPP Partner Center Admin privileges to view the workspace. Also be sure to update your MAICPP Partner Center Admin contacts to ensure we can reach you when you qualify.
