# Ordinary returns

| Metadata | Value |
|---|---|
| Source ID | KB-RET-01 |
| Version | 1.0 |
| Status | Approved |
| Owner and approver | Priya Cole |
| Effective from | 2026-09-24 |
| Review by | 2026-12-31 |
| Authority | POL-S01; replaces KB-RET-OLD |

Unused goods with proof of purchase qualify for the ordinary returns process when the request is made within **30 calendar days of purchase, inclusive**. Day 30 is included; day 31 is not. Confirm purchase date, unused condition and proof before asserting eligibility.

Missing proof, used goods, requests outside the window, or facts not supplied: explain what is missing and refer to human support where outside the ordinary rules. Do not promise a refund or invent an exception. This article does not determine statutory rights.

Damaged or unsafe products are not ordinary-returns decisions. Follow KB-SVC-01 safety escalation.
