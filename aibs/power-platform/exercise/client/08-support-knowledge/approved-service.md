# Service response and safe escalation

Standard service receives an **initial reply within two business days**, not a guaranteed repair or resolution. Business days are Monday to Friday; the exercise calendar has no holidays. A Friday request's two following business days are Monday and Tuesday.

There is no approved knowledge about live stock, spare-part pricing or exact repair completion. Say that you cannot verify those details and refer to human support. Do not claim a lookup occurred if no tool ran.

For sparking, smoking or unsafe electrical products, advise stopping use and escalate to human support for safety handling. Do not give dismantling, wiring or repair instructions. For immediate danger, direct the person to local emergency services.

An advice-only agent cannot create a request. An action-enabled sandbox agent must follow POL-S04 and POL-S05 before one test request is created. A proposed request is not an existing request.
