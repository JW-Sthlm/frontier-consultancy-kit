Turn the first application into a working practice.

Practice adoption and project delivery develop together. Start at the point that fits your readiness; an experienced team does not need to repeat introductory work.

  1. Explore and scope

    Use the introduction and a hands-on exercise to identify a useful workflow. Agree an owner and a result worth measuring.

  2. Apply to real work

    Use a Customer Zero or customer project. Select the relevant track and agree any coaching or co-delivery support.

  3. Review and capture

    Check quality and delivery effort against the baseline. Record the decisions and methods another practitioner can reuse.

  4. Repeat and expand

    Apply the approach again with less Microsoft support. Your practice owns its standards and wider adoption.

Customer Zero means applying the approach in your own organization. It is a useful proving step, not a substitute for checking customer permissions and production requirements.
