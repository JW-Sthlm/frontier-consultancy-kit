# Security and public-release policy

## Report a vulnerability or accidental disclosure

Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/JW-Sthlm/frontier-consultancy-kit/security/advisories/new) for:

- exposed credentials or tokens
- private tenant, customer, CRM, or SharePoint information
- personal local paths or document metadata
- confidential, NDA-bound, or Microsoft-internal material
- a security issue in a script or sample

Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. For non-sensitive documentation corrections, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/JW-Sthlm/frontier-consultancy-kit/issues).

## Public-release boundary

All tracked files are treated as public. Examples should use fictional names, synthetic data, placeholders, or information already approved for public use. A public scenario must not imply that partner-confidential material is included.

Before contributing:

1. Remove credentials, private URLs, tenant identifiers, personal paths, and document sensitivity metadata.
2. Replace real customer or partner details with fictional or clearly labelled composite examples.
3. Confirm that screenshots and Office files do not expose user names, recent files, local paths, or hidden metadata.
4. Check that you have the right to publish any third-party material.

Product examples are provided for workshop use. They are not production security guidance, and they may require extra controls for your environment.
