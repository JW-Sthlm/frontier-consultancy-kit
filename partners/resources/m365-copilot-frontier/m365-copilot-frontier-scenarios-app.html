AI-First Beyond Delivery - M365 Copilot for Frontier Consultancy

The Frontier Consultancy focuses on AI-first customer delivery, but an AI-first partner is AI-first everywhere. On the project side, project management, consulting, and development also contain repetitive language-heavy work where Copilot helps. The same applies across internal functions such as Finance, HR, purchasing, facilities, and sales.

Why this matters

  • Direct productivity gains - hours recovered per person per week across departments.
  • Internal credibility - teams can model AI transformation from their own practice.
  • A replication pattern - the internal adoption motion becomes the same motion used with customers.
