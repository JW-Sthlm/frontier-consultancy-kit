Why great D365 demos are hard to build

Setting up a credible demo environment is slow, manual, and rarely tailored to the customer in the room.

1–2 wks
To provision & seed a demo
Environment provisioning, sample data, security roles and configuration eat days of specialist time per opportunity.
Generic
Off-the-shelf demo data
Contoso-style datasets rarely reflect the prospect's industry, terminology or processes — so the story doesn't land.
Scarce
Skilled resources
Pre-sales consultants spend on setup the time they should spend crafting a compelling, customer-specific narrative.

What pre-sales teams are telling us

Rebuilding demo data by hand for every prospect and industry vertical.
Environments drift and break between demos — constant re-work before each meeting.
Hard to reflect a customer's real processes without deep configuration effort.
No easy way to reset, clone or share a known-good demo baseline.
